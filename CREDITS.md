below are CC0 (public domain) and free for commercial or non-commercial
use without attribution, but credit is given here anyway.

- `art/intro/characters/player/` — "Toon Characters 1" pack (male adventurer),
  used for the player's walk/idle/talk/think/interact frames.
- `art/intro/characters/npc/` — "Toon Characters 1" pack (female/male person),
  used for Mother's and Father's portrait/idle frames.
- `art/intro/environments/props/well.png`, `table.png`, `table_wide.png`,
  `chair.png` — "Roguelike/RPG Pack", sliced from `roguelikeSheet_transparent.png`.
